RallyMaster Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how H William Connors II (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use RallyMaster services, the RallyMaster web application, RallyMaster Odometer, and related RallyMaster companion applications.

Information We Handle

  • Account information. When you create a RallyMaster account, the identity provider may provide your name and email address so we can identify and support your account.
  • RallyMaster content. We store the organizations, rally information, and other content you create in RallyMaster in order to provide the service.
  • Location and sensor information. RallyMaster Odometer may access precise location, motion sensors, and Bluetooth on your device to calculate rally distance, display GPS status, and communicate with a nearby Controller device. These measurements are processed on the device for these functions and are not sent to our servers by the current RallyMaster Odometer application.
  • Technical information. Our web server may receive standard connection information, such as IP address, browser details, and request logs, to operate, secure, and diagnose the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and secure RallyMaster, maintain your account, synchronize the content you choose to store with the service, respond to support requests, and diagnose reliability or security issues.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to operate RallyMaster, such as our identity provider and hosting infrastructure, or when required by law. Those providers may use information only to provide their services to us.

Retention and Deletion

We retain account and RallyMaster content while your account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request account or data deletion by emailing hwconnors@gmail.com.

Your Choices

You can control location, Bluetooth, and motion permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related RallyMaster Odometer feature from working. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact H William Connors II at hwconnors@gmail.com.
